# Klaargesukkel — Brand Identity

## Name
Afrikaans wordplay: "klaar" (done/finished) + "gesukkel" (the struggling/hassle). Reads as
**"done with the struggle."** A business that ends everyday hassles — for people, orgs, and
businesses — through small, sharp digital solutions.

## Tagline options
- **Klaar met sukkel.** (primary, Afrikaans-market facing — .co.za)
- **Smart enough to keep it simple.** (primary English catchline — signatures, .com, anything
  international. Chosen 2026-07-29 over "No more sukkel. Just solutions." because it actually
  states the positioning — practical, smart, no unnecessary complexity — rather than just
  restating the name.)
- Other English options considered, kept as backups if the primary ever feels stale:
  "Practical. Smart. Solved." / "Sharp fixes for everyday hassles." / "Real problems. Smart
  solutions."

## Positioning
Not a single product — a growing shelf of small, focused tools (ordering systems, WhatsApp
bots, training programs, and whatever comes next) built fast and hosted properly. Each one
solves one real hassle for one real person or business.

## Voice
Direct, practical, a little cheeky. No corporate fluff. Talks like someone who actually fixed
the problem, not someone who wrote a case study about it.

## Color palette
- **Ink** `#12232E` — near-black navy, primary text/background
- **Amber** `#F2A93B` — primary accent, CTAs, energy
- **Teal** `#1F7A6C` — secondary accent, trust/tech
- **Sand** `#F7F3EC` — light background, warmth
- **Fog** `#8CA0AA` — muted text/borders

## Type
System sans stack (Inter-style) for speed and legibility everywhere — no custom font loading
needed for an MVP hub.

## Logo (v0)
Wordmark only for now: "klaargesukkel" lowercase, tight tracking, amber period/dot as accent.
A proper mark can come once the first product ships and there's real brand budget to justify it.
